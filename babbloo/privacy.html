Babbloo privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Babbloo ("the app") is a sensory play app for babies and young children, made by DreadPyke. We built it so that it collects nothing. This policy explains what that means.

The short version

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
  • We don't collect, store or share any personal information about you or your child.
  • The app works fully offline. Babbloo itself never connects to the internet: the only connection is Google Play's own, used to check and take subscription payments, which Google handles under its own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

The choices you make in the app (your child's age band, the first name you choose to enter, play mode, sound and sleep-sound settings, the playtime timer and daily limit, favourite scenes and whether the screen lock is on), the playtime and learning progress shown on the Progress screen, and whether Premium is active are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us and we can't see them. If your phone's own Android backup is switched on, Android may include them in your personal Google account backup, as it does for most apps; that backup is yours and we can't see it either. Reset app clears them, and so does uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Purchases

Babbloo Premium is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details under Google's own privacy policy; we never see them. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Premium, so it can unlock it on your devices. Purchases are behind a parent check.

Children

Babbloo is designed for children under 5, used with a parent or carer. It follows Google Play's Families policy: no ads, no data collection, no links out of the app or purchases without a parent check. We don't knowingly collect any information from children.

Screen lock

The screen lock uses Android's built-in screen pinning. It only keeps Babbloo on screen; it doesn't read or change anything else on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
