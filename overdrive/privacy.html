OVERDRIVE privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026 · OVERDRIVE: Game Booster, by DreadPyke

OVERDRIVE is a game launcher and toolkit for Android. It works without an account, and nothing you do in it is sent to us. This page explains what the app reads, why, and the one exception: ads.

What stays on your phone

  • Your games and settings. Your game list, per-game settings, protected apps, Game HUD layout, EQ, theme and language are saved on your phone only.
  • Play time (usage access). If you allow Android's usage access, OVERDRIVE reads how long each app was in the foreground to show your play time and which apps a boost stopped, and (Premium, if you turn on Autopilot or session reports) to notice which game is open. This is read on your phone and never leaves it.
  • Device readings. Memory, storage, battery, temperature, chipset, screen refresh rate and network type are read live from your phone to show them in the app. They are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Session reports and network history (Premium, if you turn them on). While a game is open, OVERDRIVE records that session’s length, battery used, peak temperature, throttling and ping, and it can check ping once a minute for your network history. These records are kept in the app’s own storage on your phone (the last 500 sessions and 7 days of network checks) and never leave it. Stats → Clear history, or uninstalling, deletes them.
  • Installed apps. OVERDRIVE lists apps that have a launcher icon so you can launch your games and choose protected apps. The list stays on your phone.

Permissions and why

  • Close other apps: Boost asks Android to stop the background processes of other apps.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): to silence notifications during a boost session or in Focus mode.
  • Display over other apps (optional): only for the Game HUD panel you turn on.
  • Notifications (optional): to show the active boost session and the Game HUD.
  • Usage access (optional): for play-time stats, as above.
  • Audio settings: for the system-wide equalizer you turn on.
  • Internet and network state: for the network check and the HUD's ping, and for ads.
  • Shizuku (optional, for Max performance in OVERDRIVE+ Mode): if you install and allow Shizuku, OVERDRIVE uses it to change Android's own game settings (performance mode, resolution, frame-rate cap) for the games you pick. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Network check

The ping test and the HUD's ping open a short connection to public cloud endpoints in several regions (Amazon Web Services) to time the round trip. No personal data is sent; like any connection, those servers see your IP address.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and app interactions to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the UK, EU and other places that require it, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Premium

OVERDRIVE Premium is a one-time purchase sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; OVERDRIVE only learns whether your Google account has bought it, so it can unlock OVERDRIVE+ Mode and turn ads off.

Children

OVERDRIVE is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Everything OVERDRIVE keeps is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.
